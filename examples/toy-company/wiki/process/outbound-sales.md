# Outbound sales

Reps hold a book of corporate accounts and choose themselves which to work each
quarter. There is no queue and no randomisation: a rep looks at the account,
forms a view about whether it is worth the effort, and acts on that view.

The CRM records the *activity* — a call happened, an email went out. It records
nothing about the judgement behind it, and nothing about how well the call was
worked. Two accounts with one logged call each can have had completely different
amounts of attention.

That unrecorded judgement is [[sales_rep_effort]], and it is why observational
comparisons of called against uncalled accounts do not answer the question the
sales director is actually asking.

## Consequence

Reps target accounts they expect to convert. So the accounts that get called are
the accounts that would have upgraded at a higher rate anyway. The naive
comparison is guaranteed to overstate the effect of calling, and no column in
the warehouse fixes it.
